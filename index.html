Buzz (the beer orb) powers your skills. Defense builds it by hitting things, Offense a bit of both, and the Coach refills it fast on his own. Five dungeons lie out in the snow, each with a boss and a treasure chest. Your first clear of each one guarantees a Legendary. Loot gets picked up when you walk over it.
